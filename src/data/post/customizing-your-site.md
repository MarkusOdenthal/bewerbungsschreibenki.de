## Making It Yours

This template is designed to be easily customizable. Here are some tips to get started.

### Branding

1. Update `src/site.config.ts` with your site name and metadata
2. Replace images in `src/assets/images/`
3. Adjust colors in your Tailwind configuration

### Navigation

Edit `src/navigation.ts` to update header and footer links.

### Content

- Update page content in `src/pages/`
- Add blog posts in `src/data/post/`
- Modify components in `src/components/`
